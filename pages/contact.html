Rémi GEORGES

Computer Music Designer, Composer, Sound Technician,
Live Coder Artist


Rémi Georges is a computer music designer, sound engineer, musician and visual artist.
His practice spans computer music composition, sound design and live performance,
and moves freely between sonic and visual media.
He is active in the Cookie Collective, TOPLAP live coding community and in the independent,
DIY and free-software oriented digital arts networks of France,
collaborating with practitioners of experimental and computer music.
Performing, working as computer music designer or hosting workshops.
He worked for institutions such as GRAME, Opéra de Lyon,
Universität der Künste Berlin, ENSAD Paris and artists such as
Vahan Soghomonian, Cie Phonème and more...
He performs live-coded music and publishes his work at remigeorges.fr and on his label Ordinateur Dans la Tête.
